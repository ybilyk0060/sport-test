Renewal and cancellation

The subscription renews automatically every 30 days at the regular price until you cancel. The price and first renewal date are shown next to the payment button. We email you 3 days before each renewal. Cancel any time in “My subscription” in one tap; access stays until the end of the paid period. See also our Terms and Refund Policy.

Prices include applicable taxes. The introductory price applies while the discount shown on the result screen is active.
