Refund & cancellation

Last updated 25 November 2026.

Cancellation

Cancel any time in “My subscription” at the bottom of the report (one button) or by writing to . No new charges after cancellation; access remains until the end of the paid period. 3 days before every renewal we email you the price, date and a cancellation link.

First payment

Before paying, you confirm that access to the report and program starts immediately. Once the full report has been opened, the service has been provided and the first payment is not refundable.

Renewals

If your subscription renewed and you didn’t manage to cancel, write to us within 7 days of the charge. We’ll refund the new period if no workout has been started in it.

Technical failures — always refunded

If the report didn’t open after payment and we couldn’t fix it, or you were charged twice, we refund the extra charge in full.

How refunds are paid

To the same card through the payment provider, usually within 3–10 business days depending on your bank. Please include the email you paid with and the charge date.
