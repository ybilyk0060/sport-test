Refund & cancellation

Last updated 25 November 2026.

Cancellation

Cancel any time in “My subscription” at the bottom of the report or by writing to . No new charges after cancellation; access remains until the end of the paid period. 3 days before every renewal we email you the price, date and a cancellation link.

14-day refunds

You can get a full refund of any payment within 14 days of the charge if you haven’t used what that payment unlocked:

  • First payment — if the full report has not been opened.
  • Renewal — if no workout has been started in the new period.

The report opens right after payment, as you confirm at checkout.

Technical failures — always refunded

If the report didn’t open after payment and we couldn’t fix it, or you were charged twice, we refund the extra charge in full.

How to request

Write to with the email you paid with and the charge date. Refunds go back to the same card, usually within 3–10 business days depending on your bank.

Refunds are processed by Paddle.com, our Merchant of Record.

This policy does not limit any rights you have under consumer protection law in your country.
